Founded on 28 March 1990 by Late Shri R. B. Rastogi and his family in memory of Late Lala Narain Dass Rastogi, the school in Mohalla Jhanda, Kiratpur (District Bijnor, U.P.) began with 9 LKG pupils. Today it runs a co-educational primary school and a girls' Junior High and Inter College up to Class XII, under the Narain Dass Rastogi Charitable Trust.

स्व. लाला नारायण दास रस्तोगी की पुण्य स्मृति में स्व. श्री आर. बी. रस्तोगी एवं परिवार द्वारा 28 मार्च 1990 को स्थापित यह विद्यालय मोहल्ला झण्डा, कीरतपुर (जिला बिजनौर) में मात्र 9 शिशुओं से आरम्भ हुआ। आज यहाँ प्राथमिक विद्यालय तथा कक्षा 12 तक बालिका जूनियर हाईस्कूल एवं इण्टर कॉलेज संचालित हैं।

Pillar 01 • Girls' Education

A safe path to Class XII for the daughters of Kiratpur

Founder's Tribute

Late Shri R. B. Rastogi

1932 – 2003 • Educationist • S/o Late Shri Badri Parshad

Born in Kiratpur on 12 December 1932, Late Shri R. B. Rastogi moved to Delhi with his father at the age of ten and completed an M.A. in English in 1952. He taught English at Gandhi Inter College, Dhanaura from 1953 to 1960, and then served as a principal from 1960 to 1985.

After retirement he gave his life to religious and public-welfare work. With his brothers he founded the school in Kiratpur on 28 March 1990, named after his grandfather Late Lala Narain Dass Rastogi, and remained the lifelong head of the Trust. He passed away in Dubai on 9 February 2003. He was married to Smt. Shakuntala Devi.

36-Year Journey

Key Milestones (1990 – 2026)

1990

School founded with 9 pupils

Founded on 28 March 1990 (Chaitra Sudi 2, Samvat 2047). The first session began on 18 July 1990 with 9 children in LKG, a monthly fee of ₹25 and three rooms. Shri Yogeshwar Bhatnagar was the first principal, with Smt. Usha Bhatnagar as assistant teacher.

1991

Narain Dass Rastogi Charitable Trust registered

Registered in Delhi on 09 August 1991 as a public charitable trust for education and social welfare; the Trust runs the Kiratpur schools.

1992–93

Management committee, more rooms and land

A School Management Committee was formed in January 1992. In 1993 the founder built four more rooms and the adjoining land was purchased for the growing school.

1996–97

Permanent recognition of the primary school

Permanent recognition for the primary section from the Basic Shiksha Parishad.

2002

Girls' Junior High School (Classes VI – VIII)

At parents' strong request, Junior High recognition was obtained from the Basic Shiksha Parishad to take girls' education further.

2004

High School recognition (UP Board)

Through the staff's dedication, Madhyamik Shiksha Parishad, U.P. granted High School recognition. Smt. Usha Bhatnagar took charge as principal.

2005

Physics & Chemistry labs

Shri Ravinder Kumar Rastogi (Delhi) built the physics and chemistry laboratories and two classrooms.

2006

Intermediate (Classes XI – XII) recognition

After sustained effort by the staff, the college received Intermediate recognition. The then MLA, Shri Kunwar Bharatendu Singh, gave ₹1 lakh from his MLA fund for the science laboratory.

2010–11

‘A’ grade from the UP Board

Madhyamik Shiksha Parishad, U.P. awarded the college an ‘A’ grade for excellent Intermediate results in 2010 and 2011.

2014

Peak enrolment of 591 students

Enrolment grew from 9 in 1990 to 591 in 2014. Between 2008 and 2023 the campus also received solar lights twice from Teachers' MLC Dr. Hari Singh Dhillon.

2026

Smart class, new principal and fresh registrations

A smart TV was donated by Shri Umesh Gupta (Pune) on 1 April 2026 and the smart class was launched in July. Smt. Usha Bhatnagar, who served the school from its first session in 1990 and as principal from 2004, completed her tenure on 31 July 2026 with the school's gratitude. Shri Devendra Singh became principal on 1 August 2026. The Trust received provisional income-tax registration and 80G approval (June 2026), CSR-1 registration and an NGO Darpan ID.

Founding Charter • 09 August 1991

From Ancestral Bijnor Roots to an Enduring Temple of Learning पैतृक भूमि से शिक्षा के पावन मन्दिर तक की यात्रा

On 09 August 1991, Late Shri Ram Bahadur Gupta (R.B. Gupta / Ram Bahadur Rastogi), together with his brothers Late Shri Raj Bahadur Rastogi and Late Shri Satish Chandra Rastogi (sons of Late Shri Badri Parshad), formally registered the Narain Dass Rastogi Charitable Trust in Delhi (Registration Nos. 3315 & 1812). Alongside them stood the original 1991 Founding Trustees: Smt. Shakuntala Devi, Shri Ravinder Kumar Rastogi, Shri Suresh Rastogi, and Shri Manoj Rastogi.

Moved by the lack of quality schooling for girls and underprivileged children in their ancestral town of Kiratpur (District Bijnor, U.P.), the founders dedicated their ancestral property in Mohalla Jhanda, Kiratpur to establish Shri Narayan Das Rastogi Saraswati Shishu Mandir—which grew into Balika Vidya Mandir Junior High School and Balika Vidya Mandir Inter College.

"Shri Narain Dass Rastogi Saraswati Shishu Mandir at Mohalla Jhanda, Kiratpur, Distt. Bijnor (U.P.) is built at the ancestral property of the settlors of the Trust... Free education is being imparted to the poor students while a nominal fee of Rs. 45/- p.m. is being charged from other students... Every year a medical check-up of the students is conducted by a team of voluntary doctors from Delhi & surrounding areas, and a boring well has been dug in the school premises providing drinking water to the students and villagers."
— Excerpt from Official Trust Certificate dated 04 October 1993, signed by Founder President Late Shri R.B. Gupta

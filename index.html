Mission & Architecture

Built for long-term rural transformation, not short-term charity.

Every rupee contributed by the family and partner donors goes directly into classroom teaching, girl-child retention, clean drinking water, and digital readiness in District Bijnor.

Pillar 01 • Girl-Child Empowerment

Closing the secondary-school dropout gap for daughters of Kiratpur

In rural Bijnor, many girls historically paused schooling after Class V due to distance and cost. By establishing a dedicated Balika Vidya Mandir Junior High and UP Board High School right inside Mohalla Jhanda, the Trust provides a safe, dignified academic pathway through Class X at nominal or zero fee for deserving families.

• Safe Neighbourhood Campus • Fee Waivers for Meritorious & EWS Students • Dedicated Female Educators
Pillar 02 • 2026 Campus Revival

Modernizing infrastructure for a new generation

Under the reconstituted 2026 Board of Trustees, the campus is undergoing targeted upgrades: purified RO water stations, renovated girls' sanitation blocks, science & computer lab modernisation, and structured teacher development.

Founder's Tribute

Late Shri R. B. Rastogi

Founder President (1991) • S/o Late Shri Badri Parshad

"True prosperity lies in lighting the lamp of knowledge for every child in our ancestral hometown—ensuring that no daughter or son of Kiratpur is denied education for want of means."

On 09 August 1991, Late Shri R. B. Rastogi—alongside Co-Founders Late Shri Raj Bahadur Rastogi and Late Shri Satish Chandra Rastogi—instituted the Narain Dass Rastogi Charitable Trust in Delhi in sacred memory of his grandfather, Late Lala Narain Dass Rastogi.

Supported by the Founding Patron Family including Smt. Shakuntala Devi (W/o Late Shri R. B. Rastogi), he anchored the Trust's flagship educational mission in Mohalla Jhanda, Kiratpur—building an institution that continues to thrive across three generations of family stewardship.

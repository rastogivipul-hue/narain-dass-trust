June 2026जून 2026

Provisional 80G approval and income-tax registrationअस्थायी 80G स्वीकृति और आयकर पंजीकरण

On 25 June 2026 the Trust received provisional registration (12AB-equivalent, AAATN1994LE20261) and provisional approval for donor deduction (80G-equivalent, AAATN1994LF20261) under the Income-tax Act, 2025, valid for tax years 2026–27 to 2028–29.
